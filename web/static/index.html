EloSight Play

How the rating estimate works

While you play, a model guesses both players' ratings from the game alone: the moves so far, how long each move took and how much time is left. It never sees who you are.

Training

It was trained on rated blitz games from the Lichess open database (CC0). For every half-move of every game it learned to predict both players' Lichess blitz ratings. Players used to measure accuracy were kept out of training entirely.

The numbers

Next to each name is the current estimate and, after the ±, the half-width of an 80% range: on test games, the true rating fell inside that range about 8 times in 10. Expect it to start wide and narrow as the game goes on. The chart shows the estimate after every half-move; click it, or a move, to step back through the game.

Limits

Ratings are Lichess Glicko-2 blitz ratings, not FIDE Elo. Lichess pairs players of similar strength, so when two very different players meet here the model tends to pull their estimates toward each other. Only blitz time controls are offered because that is all it was trained on.
