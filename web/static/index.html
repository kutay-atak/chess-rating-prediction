EloSight Play

How the game performance works

The number next to each name is how strong that player has played in this game so far, as a Lichess blitz rating. It depends only on their own moves, so a blunder pulls down the player who made it and leaves the opponent's number alone.

Opening

While the position is one that many Lichess players have reached (by any move order), the estimate leans on the average rating of the players who reached it.

After the opening

Stockfish scores every move. From how much winning chance each move gave away, every move gets an accuracy from 0 to 100, and each player's running accuracy is compared with real Lichess games: the number is the median rating of players who had that accuracy after that many moves, and the ± range covers the middle 80% of them.

Without Stockfish

If Stockfish isn't installed where the site runs, the panel says so and the numbers come from a model that guesses each player's rating from the moves and clocks instead.

Limits

Ratings are Lichess Glicko-2 blitz ratings, not FIDE Elo, from rated blitz games in the Lichess open database (CC0). One game is a small sample: early numbers are rough.
